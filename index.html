Ghousia Nalli Biryani — Karachi's Original Beef Nalli Biryani in Liaquatabad

Ghousia Nalli Biryani is a single, original beef nalli biryani kitchen at Main Sarafa Bazar, Liaquatabad Town, Karachi — bone-in beef shank cooked low and slow until the marrow melts into the gravy. We're open Monday to Saturday, 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM, with delivery across Karachi from 12:30 PM to 10:00 PM (closed Sundays). See the full menu below, then order in seconds by phone or WhatsApp.
